# Head of Customer Success

Ton Head of Customer Success dans Claude. Onboarding + Health Score + entretiens + lifecycle email.

## Quand l'appeler

- Tu structures ton onboarding client (S1 / M1)
- Tu mets en place un Health Score
- Tu fais des entretiens de rétention
- Tu construis le lifecycle email post-vente

## Compétences (skills bundlées)

| Skill | Rôle |
|---|---|
| `customer-success` | Onboarding + Health Score |
| `customer-interview` | Entretiens JTBD clients |
| `email-campaign` | Lifecycle email |

## Usage

```
/customer-success
```

4 phases : brief → onboarding & Health Score → entretiens → email lifecycle → pack CS.
